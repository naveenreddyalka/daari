  batches, files, responses, virtual keys/teams, the usage ledger, and the audit log
  default to per-pod SQLite — GETs can 404, SSO keys 401 on other pods, counters split,
  and `daari audit export` is incomplete across replicas.

  DAARI_BATCHES__BACKEND / DAARI_FILES__BACKEND / DAARI_RESPONSES__BACKEND /
  DAARI_SERVER__VIRTUAL_KEYS__BACKEND / DAARI_OBSERVABILITY__BACKEND /
  DAARI_ENTERPRISE__AUDIT_BACKEND to postgres, or keep
  replicaCount and autoscaling.minReplicas at 1. autoscaling.keda.minReplicaCount
  is refused above 1 until postgres is enabled. See docs/developer/guides/features/batches.md.
